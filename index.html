    On the road

    What we found out about cooking and shopping around Tasmania in November and December.

    ⛴️ Crossing on the Spirit

    Fresh fruit and vegetables can't come into Tasmania, including potatoes, fresh herbs, garlic, ginger and onions. Dried pulses, pasta, rice, flour, dried herbs and spices, sauces and oils are fine.

    Honey is on the "if in doubt, leave it at home" list, so buy local leatherwood instead. Food eaten on board has to be binned before you leave the terminal.

    🔁 The restock rhythm

    1. Shop daySalmon or fish that night. It's the most perishable thing you'll buy.
    2. Night 2Chicken or mince.
    3. Nights 3–4Mushrooms, spinach, spring onions and cucumber, before they tire.
    4. Nights 5–7Pantry one-pots on hardy veg: cabbage, carrots, pumpkin, potatoes, onions.
    5. RescueChorizo stew or fried rice if the next shop slips a few days.

    🛒 Where to restock

    Woolworths stores cluster around Hobart, Launceston and the north. The west coast towns (Queenstown, Strahan, Zeehan) are small, so stock up for a longer stretch before heading west.

    Plan a shop soon after you roll off the ferry, since you'll arrive with no fresh food.

    🧊 Packing the fridge and drawers

    • Fridge at 3–4°C. Raw meat sealed, on the bottom.
    • Leftovers: cool them, get them in the fridge within 2 hours, eat within 2–3 days, and reheat until steaming. Be strictest with rice.
    • Hardy veg in breathable bags in a drawer, not plastic. Keep potatoes away from onions.
    • Tins low and heavy so they can't crush anything. Eggs in a hard case. Avocados rock-hard.
    • Potatoes last two to three months in cool weather, carrots one to two weeks or more, and garlic for months. Cabbage keeps for weeks, so use it where you'd use lettuce.

    🔥 Gas and fire bans

    Seasonal campfire bans usually start in late spring or summer, but gas stoves and gas barbecues are still permitted. Some areas, including the World Heritage Area and Freycinet, are fuel-stove-only all year.

    On Total Fire Ban days, check the Tasmania Fire Service before cooking outdoors.

    🍓 In season, Nov to Dec

    Local strawberries start hitting shelves in November and the first cherries are expected from mid-December. Grab the free Tasmanian Seasonal Produce Guide on the Spirit or at a visitor centre to find farm gates.

    Easy local swaps: Tasmanian salmon, Tassie cheddar and leatherwood honey.

    🧽 Less washing up

    • Wipe pans with paper towel before washing.
    • One Jetboil of hot water does the dishes for two.
    • Heat rice pouches first, then cook in the same pan.
